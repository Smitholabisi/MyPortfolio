Tableau Projects

This project focuses on the Airbnb dataset, which includes calendar data, listings, and reviews. The objective is to optimize the pricing of new house listings by analyzing how other properties in the surrounding areas are priced. This analysis considers factors such as location, amenities, reviews, and the number of beds.

The project involves:
- Cleaning and pre-processing the dataset
- Data visualization

One of the initial steps in Tableau was to load the dataset and merge the tables. The dataset was then cleaned and pre-processed by addressing missing values, removing duplicates, and converting data into appropriate formats.

Various visualizations were considered for this project, including maps, line charts, and bar charts. Additionally, sorting and filtering were applied to the dataset.

KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) reveal the following:
a. The highest revenue recorded in a single year was $2,110,350 in December 2016.
b. The average price by Zipcode 98134 is the highest at $206.6, while the lowest average price by zipcode is $64.7.
c. The distinct count of bedroom listings: 1-bedroom units total 1,811, while 6-bedroom units total 5.
d. The average price per bedroom shows a range: the lowest average price is $96.2, and the highest is $584.8.
